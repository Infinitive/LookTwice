# Writing guide

The voice is first-person, direct, and careful. The work should feel lived-in without becoming grandiose or over-explained.

## Tone

- clear, not ornamental
- honest, not self-conscious
- specific rather than generic
- grounded in lived detail

## Structure

For Stories, use section headings when the piece moves across time, place, or theme.

Example:

- Wichita
- Louisiana
- Returning to Kansas
- Diagnosis
- The Military
- Marriage
- Dallas
- Sobriety
- Home

Use pull quotes sparingly. They should feel like a sentence that is worth stopping on, not a decorative flourish.

## Privacy and boundaries

The publication should avoid exposing personal information that would create risk for another person or a family member. Use names only when the context is intentional and safe. Keep the focus on the writer's experience rather than on exposing sensitive details about others.
