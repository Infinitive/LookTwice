# Content model

LookTwice uses three primary content types.

## Story

Story entries are the long-form essays and retrospective pieces. They usually have a strong timeline, a place, and a major emotional or intellectual arc.

Use Story for:

- memoir-ish pieces
- essays about identity, place, work, trauma, or transformation
- pieces that can justify section headings and a larger reading width

## Notes

Notes are shorter and looser. They can be finished thoughts, observations, or unfinished fragments.

Use Notes for:

- prompt-like entries
- quick essays
- observations that are not yet fully shaped

## Things

Things is a catalogue of works and activities that keep returning in the life of the project. Each entry belongs to one of three editorial categories:

- Watching: films, anime, and television
- Listening: albums, artists, and music
- Doing: games, projects, activities, and other things that involve action

Each collection follows the same metadata rules. The differences are in the shape of the content and the kinds of relationships it supports.
