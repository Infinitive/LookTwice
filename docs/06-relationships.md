# Relationships

Relationships are not a dump of everything someone liked. They are a curated list of what should be read next.

## Rule 1: connect only what is meaningfully adjacent

A relationship should make sense in five years, not just today.

Good examples:

- memory and place
- systems and institutions
- life changes and their aftermath

Weak examples:

- "I like both of these"
- toys on the same shelf
- random topical overlap

## Rule 2: keep it small

Aim for 3-5 related entries. Avoid huge lists that dilute the point.

## Rule 3: explain a relationship

If you cannot finish the sentence "If someone liked this, they should read..." then the relationship is probably not strong enough.

Use related entries to deepen a thread, not to produce a sprawling web.
